Availability

Downloads

The website is public. Standalone QCA and the comprehensive Viewer are available only after GitHub sign-in as the authorized mt-hagar account. There is no HAGRad registration or invitation service.

Both apps run locally and keep DICOM files on your computer. The Viewer includes QCA. Choose a macOS DMG or Windows EXE below.

QCA for Apple Silicon macOS

HAGRad-QCA-macOS.dmg. This Developer ID-unsigned research preview may trigger Gatekeeper.

  1. Open the DMG.
  2. Double-click HAGRad QCA.app.
  3. First launch only: if macOS blocks the unsigned app, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, then choose Open Anyway for HAGRad QCA and confirm.

Get macOS QCA

QCA for Windows

HAGRad-QCA-Windows.exe. This research-preview build is unsigned.

  1. Open HAGRad-QCA-Windows.exe.
  2. First launch only: if Windows SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway only for the package obtained from the private QCA release.

The QCA executable starts a local server and opens the workflow in your browser.

Get Windows QCA

Viewer for Apple Silicon macOS

HAGRad-Viewer-macOS.dmg includes QCA and the integrated HAGRad workflows. This research preview is unsigned.

  1. Open the DMG and drag HAGRad Viewer.app to Applications.
  2. Open the app. If Gatekeeper blocks it, choose Open Anyway in Privacy & Security.

Get macOS Viewer

Our mission

Heart Analysis Gateway for Radiologic Research and Development

Heart Analysis Gateway for Radiologic Research and Development (HAGRad) was developed as a research platform founded on the belief that advanced radiology research and education can become more accessible, reproducible, collaborative, and engaging. Built through more than 1,000 hours of development, refinement, debugging, testing, and real-world research use, the platform was designed to inspire and empower the next generation of researchers, physicians, engineers, and students by transforming complex image analysis into structured, supervised, and educationally meaningful research experiences.

Through synchronized reconstruction comparison, quantitative quality-control workflows, exportable structured outputs, and transparent analytical pipelines, the platform aims to modernize mentoring, facilitate scientific onboarding, and foster reproducible research practices within radiology and imaging science. By combining local image analysis with educational and research-oriented workflow design, the project seeks to accelerate innovation in imaging science while strengthening mentorship, collaboration, and long-term engagement in academic radiology and imaging research.
